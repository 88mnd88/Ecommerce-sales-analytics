# E-Commerce Sales Analytics & Customer Segmentation Dashboard (MS Excel / WPS Office)
Interactive Excel/WPS dashboard analyzing 5,000+ e-commerce transactions. Features advanced text parsing, cohort segmentation, and dynamic multi-slicer reporting.

## Project Overview
This pet project focuses on a comprehensive end-to-end data analysis of an e-commerce platform's operational performance. The dataset contains 5,000 transaction records spanning from 2022 to 2026. 

The goal of the project was to clean raw transactional data, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA), engineer new business metrics, and build an interactive dashboard to uncover sales trends, geographical performance, and customer satisfaction issues.

---

## Key Business Insights (Executive Summary)
* **The Revenue Driver:** The *Electronics* category stands out as the primary revenue stream, contributing over 38% of total platform sales. 
* **The Quality Paradox (Critical Insight):** Around 50% of the total revenue in all categories is generated by products rated as **BAD** by customers. This indicates a severe product quality or logistics issue that poses a high risk.
* **The Electronics Growth Trend (2025 Focus):** In 2025, female customers generated a significantly higher share of revenue within the Electronics segment compared to previous periods. Recommendation: Conduct further research to isolate the exact drivers behind this sudden growth (e.g., specific marketing campaigns, localized product shifts). Determine if these drivers can be replicated to proactively boost revenue in current and future quarters.
* **The Demographic Shift:** In the *Home* segment, male customers consistently generate significantly higher revenue than female customers across all years. *Recommendation:* Reallocate marketing budgets to target male demographics for home improvement campaigns.
* **Geographical Hubs:** Sales are heavily concentrated in **Budapest** and **Milan**. Supply chain and warehouse scaling should be prioritized in these regional hubs.

## Technical Workflow & ETL (Extract, Transform, Load)

### 1. Data Cleaning & Anomalies Resolution
* **Date Format Harmonization:** Resolved a critical data corruption issue where dates were split into conflicting formats (`DD-MM-YYYY` and `MM-DD-YYYY`). Implemented nested string functions to standardize the entire timeline.
* **Synthetic Data Correction:** Identified and fixed a timeline anomaly where future transactions were logged up to the year 2035. Restructured the chronological sequence to simulate realistic, multi-order daily transactions.

### 2. Data Enrichment & Engineering
* **Relational Data Merging:** Used advanced lookup formula (`XLOOKUP`) to blend customer profile tables with the core transactions dataset.
* **Cohort Segmentation:** Formulated custom conditional boundaries to segment customers into automatic tier categories (`GOOD`, `OK`, `BAD`).
* **Financial Metric Engineering:** Calculated a standalone `Total Before Discount` metric to evaluate the financial leakage caused by personal discounts.

### 3. Cross-Platform Dashboard Architecture (WPS Office Optimization)
* Built a dynamic analytical reporting layer utilizing multiple relational **Pivot Tables** and **Pivot Charts**.
* **Report Connections Bypass:** Overcame WPS Office's technical limitations regarding cross-cache Slicer isolation by implementing a custom **Cascade Filtering Architecture**. Slicers dynamically trigger a master table, which broadcasts parameters to isolated charts. This successfully locked chart color-coding (Red/Green UI) during active filtering.
